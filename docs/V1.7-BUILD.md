# Scoutline Version 1.7

Version 1.7 adds two focused matchday improvements while preserving the Version 1.6 observation and export workflow.

## Goalkeeper observations

- Nine-zone save location.
- Seven-zone shot origin: left, centre and right inside the penalty area; wide left and wide right; outside-box left and outside-box right.
- Outcome selection: save, goal, parry, claim, punch or other.
- Optional dictated or typed note retained at the observation's match time.
- Save location, shot origin and outcome included as separate structured-export fields.

## Player identification and placement

- One solid identification colour per team.
- Ten simple colour choices with manual override.
- A visible warning when both teams use the same selected colour.
- An explicit **Now add Team 2** transition after Team 1 placement.
- Team 1 is temporarily hidden only after that transition is selected.
- Both teams reappear after Team 2 is confirmed for overlap adjustment and final placement confirmation.

The colours are visual aids for live player selection. They do not claim to reproduce the teams' match kits.
